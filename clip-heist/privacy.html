Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clip Heist is built so that we collect as little about you as possible.

Your videos

Your footage and the clips you make are processed and stored only on your own device, in your browser’s storage. They are never uploaded to us.

What we collect

  • Email updates: your email address, if you sign up for updates, used only to send you Clip Heist news. Unsubscribe anytime.
  • Pro: your email address and Stripe customer ID, to check your subscription and let you restore Pro on another device.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe, the merchant of record. We never see or store your card details; we receive your email, plan and subscription status.

Analytics

We don’t use advertising trackers. We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to count visits.

Sharing

We don’t sell your data. We only share it with the services needed to run Clip Heist (email delivery, sign-in and payments).

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: support@getclipheist.app. Deleting the app’s data from your browser removes your videos and clips from your device.

Children

Clip Heist isn’t directed at children under 13.
