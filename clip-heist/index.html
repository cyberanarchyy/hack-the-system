FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work with GTA 6?

Yes. Clip Heist works with footage from any game, and it shines with fast, chaotic open-world gameplay: chases, crashes, explosions and everything that goes wrong in the best way. It works just as well with shooters, racing games and stream VODs.

Can I clip other streamers?

Yes, as long as you have permission. Lots of streamers run clipping programs and pay clippers based on views. Clip Heist’s streamer layout and full-VOD scanning are built for exactly that.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Clip Heist processes everything on your own device. Your footage never touches our servers, which also means there are no upload waits or storage limits.

What do I need to run it?

A modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on PC, Mac, iPhone or Android. Nothing to install. A downloadable PC app with faster exports is coming.

Which file types work?

MP4, MOV and WebM work everywhere. OBS’s MKV recordings open in Chrome and Edge. If a file won’t open, remux it to MP4 in OBS (File → Remux Recordings).

Where can I post my clips?

Anywhere vertical video lives: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, X and Snapchat. Clips export at 9:16 in up to 1080p / 60 fps.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Cancel in a couple of clicks and you keep Pro until the end of your billing period. You can always go back to the free plan.
