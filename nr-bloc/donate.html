Support the Bloc

We don't have any backers or council funding. The space runs on donations and the time people put in. Down the line we'll apply for grants we're entitled to as a community space, and we want to eventually pay people properly for the work they do here. For now it's on us.

Where it goes

Everything goes back into keeping the space open and the community running.

Rent and bills

The biggest cost by far. If we can't pay rent there's no space, so this always comes first.

Events and materials

Keeping things free means covering costs ourselves. Printing, materials, equipment, whatever's needed.

Mutual aid

Sometimes people in the community need practical support. We help where we can, no questions asked.

Print and zines

Photocopying, printing, getting stuff out into the world. Old school but it works.
