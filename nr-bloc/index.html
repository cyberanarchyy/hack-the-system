What we do

We're a free space for left groups, activists and anyone who needs somewhere to meet or organise without it costing anything.

The space

Groups and individuals can use the space for free. Meetings, workshops, socials, film nights, whatever you need. Just get in touch.

Who's welcome

Anarchists, socialists, trade unionists, environmentalists, feminists, anti-fascists. If you're on the left and you need somewhere to be, this is it.

How it works

We don't have any funding coming in from councils or businesses. We exist because people support us directly and volunteer their time.

Get involved

Volunteer, run a workshop, donate, tell people we exist. There's always something useful you can do even if it's just showing up.
